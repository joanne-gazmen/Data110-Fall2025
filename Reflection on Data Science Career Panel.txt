2. Reflection: How does this connect to your own career goals or interests in data science?

I think the idea that there is a flexibility with having different majors while still connecting to data science is relieving, I think this because the diversity is what keeps it real. Without the versatility, there wouldn't be much to work with. So having people with different background and interest is a positive, and one of the biggest takes away for me when thinking about being a data scientist. I also feel as though I resonated with the panelist, as some of them didn't really know what they wanted to do in life, and to hear that becoming a data scientist was something life changing was nice to hear. I also resonate with the idea of thinking deeply and always asking questions. I found this to be a positive now because as a data scientist, its good to always have questions, because the more questions you have, the more you have to do to research, and that is the fun of it.
